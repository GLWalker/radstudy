## Source corrections and limitations

- **Lower Extremity, slide 63:** The subpubic-angle comparison in the lecturer table has the inequalities reversed: the typical female pubic arch has a **larger**, not smaller, subpubic angle. The incorrect sign comparison is excluded from graded questions. For independent confirmation see OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology, Section 8.3, *The Pelvic Girdle and Pelvis* (https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/8-3-the-pelvic-girdle-and-pelvis).
- Chapters 1–2 slides 2–8 contain unrelated personal photographs; they are intentionally not reproduced in the Study Center visuals.
- Chapter 13, Part I (Upper Extremities) was supplied as a legacy `.ppt`; when numerical slide positions were not reliably extractable, notes use the instructor’s visible topic headings instead of inventing slide numbers.
- Chapter 1, slide 10 describes laws as high-confidence theories. This is the instructor’s simplified wording; it is not used to quiz students on a formal hierarchy of scientific theory versus law.
- Notes preserve instructor terminology where supported; clarifying language is explicitly marked. Original questions are newly authored review prompts, not copies of instructor assessments.
