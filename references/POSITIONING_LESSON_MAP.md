## Instructor-slide corrections and clarifications

- Chapter 1 Lecture 3, slide 2: `mA × ms = mAs` requires conversion to seconds. Correct: mAs = mA × time(s) = mA × time(ms) ÷ 1000.
- Chapter 1 Lecture 3, slides 6–8: 15% kVp rule approximates detector exposure; displayed digital brightness is usually processed separately.
- Chapter 1 Lecture 3, slide 23: exposure indicator alone does not prove the patient received a minimal dose or a diagnostically acceptable image.
- Chapter 1 Lecture 4, slide 6: 100 R × 0.000258 C/kg/R = 0.0258 C/kg, not 0.258 C/kg. Air kerma, absorbed dose and equivalent dose are not universally numerically identical.
- Chapter 1 Lecture 4, slides 8, 10–14 and Chapter 4 Lecture 2 slide 2: shielding and dose-limit teaching slides were authored around 2018. Follow current regulations, RSO and facility protocols rather than treating them as operating policy.
- Chapter 1 Lecture 4 file is numbered Lecture 4, but its first slide internally says “Lecture 3.” This pack uses the filename sequence to avoid duplicate lecture numbers.
- Chapter 4 Lecture 1 slides are strongly image-based; hand bone counts and anatomical joint abbreviations are standard clarifying context for studying diagrams, rather than literal quotations.
